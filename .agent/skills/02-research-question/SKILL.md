# 02 Research Question

## Purpose

Make one scientific direction decision. The product is a retained, revised,
merged, investigated, or rejected research question and a small claim tree that
can be tested.

## Workflow

1. Define the research boundary:

   ```text
   Object, Environment, Observation, Task, Failure, Desired understanding
   ```

2. State the observed failure or unresolved contradiction that motivates the
   direction.
3. Identify the strongest verified nearby approach and its key assumption.
4. Compare mechanisms, not terminology, module names, or paper counts.
5. Write the favored explanation and strongest competing explanation.
6. Derive one observable prediction and one result that would reject the favored
   idea.
7. Define the smallest experiment or analysis that distinguishes the
   explanations under a fair comparison.
8. Build only the claims needed for the paper, for example:

   ```text
   C1: the baseline failure exists.
   C2: the failure is associated with mechanism Z.
   C3: intervention M changes Z.
   C4: task/property behavior changes accordingly.
   C5: the advantage weakens when assumption A is broken.
   ```

9. Mark each claim as theory-supported, experiment-supported,
   literature-supported, or hypothesis-only.
10. Recommend retain, revise, merge, investigate, or eliminate, and state the
    next substantive action.

## Output Contract

Return:

- revised research question or rejection decision;
- real-world/scientific failure and desired understanding;
- non-trivial difference from the closest alternative;
- competing explanations and divergent prediction;
- concise claim tree with support type;
- decisive experiment and rejection condition;
- next substantive research action.

## Boundaries

- Do not substitute scoring tables, gate reports, taxonomy labels, prior-card
  quotas, or long novelty prose for the scientific comparison.
- Do not treat “few studies,” a new name, module, loss, scale, or dataset swap as
  sufficient novelty.
- Do not claim a hypothesis-only statement as established fact.
- Do not use Python to score idea quality or enumerate formal combinations.
- Do not require hashes, evidence packages, or approval records before the
  question can be explored.
- The author makes the final direction decision; exploration itself does not
  require a ceremony.
