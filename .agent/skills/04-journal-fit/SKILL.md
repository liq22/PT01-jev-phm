# 04 Journal Fit

## Purpose

Make a venue decision that changes the manuscript or submission strategy. Do not
produce a generic ranking report.

## Workflow

1. Summarize the paper's question, contribution, evidence type, audience, and
   current maturity.
2. Verify current official scope, article types, limits, and submission rules.
3. Inspect a small set of recent comparable papers from the venue.
4. Identify the strongest fit and the most likely mismatch.
5. Recommend target, backup, or defer, and state the manuscript changes required
   for that decision.

## Output Contract

Return a concise venue recommendation with:

- why the paper belongs or does not belong;
- the closest recent venue examples;
- the article type and practical constraints;
- the substantive changes needed before submission.

## Boundaries

- Do not rank many venues without a decision criterion.
- Do not optimize formatting before confirming scope and contribution fit.
- Do not infer current policies from memory; verify official sources.
- Do not use Python to score venue fit or generate decorative comparison tables.
- Do not create policy hashes or audit packages.
