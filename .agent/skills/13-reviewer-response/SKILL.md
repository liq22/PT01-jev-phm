# 13 Reviewer Response

## Purpose

Resolve reviewer concerns in the science and manuscript, then explain the actual
change. The revised paper and response letter are the products; a response matrix
or diplomatic wording cannot substitute for the correction.

## Workflow

For each comment:

```text
reviewer concern
-> underlying scientific or editorial issue
-> root cause
-> required evidence or reasoning
-> experiment, theory, analysis, or rewrite
-> observed result
-> claim update
-> response
```

1. Restate the reviewer concern accurately and identify whether it targets
   novelty, theory, method, experiment, statistics, generalization, writing, or
   venue requirements.
2. Decide whether to add evidence, correct the method/analysis, narrow the claim,
   clarify the text, disagree with evidence, or acknowledge a limitation.
3. Modify the manuscript or run the required scientific work first.
4. Inspect the actual result. If it weakens or rejects the original claim, change
   the claim and narrative rather than defending the old story.
5. Draft a direct response containing:
   - answer;
   - change made;
   - actual result/source/reasoning;
   - manuscript location;
   - remaining boundary when relevant.
6. Verify that every promised change exists and no experiment, citation, or claim
   was invented.
7. Maintain comment IDs or a response matrix only when the volume of real
   comments makes coordination useful.

## Output Contract

Produce:

- revised manuscript passages or files;
- professional point-by-point responses;
- actual new evidence or analysis when performed;
- revised claim strength and boundary;
- one unresolved author decision only when genuinely necessary.

## Boundaries

- Do not prioritize response matrices, coverage reports, reviewer ledgers,
  diplomacy, or formatting over the manuscript correction.
- Do not claim new experiments, statistics, source checks, or analyses that were
  not performed.
- Do not preserve the original claim by reinterpreting contradictory evidence.
- Do not create speculative defenses against reviewer objections that were not
  raised and do not affect the conclusion.
- Do not use Python to score response completeness or style.
- Do not create hashes, receipts, provenance packages, or review-audit trails.
- Minor wording comments remain minor; do not let them displace unresolved
  scientific P0/P1 issues.
