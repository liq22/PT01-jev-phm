# PPTX

## Purpose

Produce an editable deck that communicates the requested content clearly. The
story, slide content, and usable `.pptx` are the product.

## Workflow

1. Fix the audience, purpose, duration, and one takeaway.
2. Select the minimum content, results, figures, and citations needed.
3. Build a coherent slide sequence before choosing a theme.
4. Create or edit the deck, preserving supplied templates when useful.
5. Use one main idea per slide and only the visual/text needed to support it.
6. Render or open the deck once and inspect affected slides for factual errors,
   overflow, unreadable text, broken images, and incorrect order.
7. Repair concrete issues and stop.

## Output Contract

Produce the editable `.pptx` and, when useful, a PDF preview. Speaker notes,
alternative themes, or slide images are optional and require a stated need.

## Boundaries

- Do not begin with palette, typography, icons, layout variants, or animation
  before the story and evidence are selected.
- Do not make every slide visually dense or force a figure where a short statement
  is clearer.
- Do not run repeated thumbnail/render review cycles without a concrete defect.
- Do not create audit logs, design reports, or multiple themes by default.
- Use code only to create or repair the actual deck, not to score style or prove
  completion.
- Stop when the deck is accurate, readable, editable, and fit for its stated use.
