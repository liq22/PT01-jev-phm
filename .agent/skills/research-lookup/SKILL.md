# Research Lookup

## Purpose

Answer a bounded current-information question using live, citable sources outside
or across scholarly databases. This Skill gathers evidence; it does not own the
research question, literature synthesis, or manuscript conclusion.

## Workflow

1. Define the exact fact, comparison, time window, jurisdiction, standard, or
   technical question that must be resolved.
2. Choose the most authoritative available source class for that question:
   official documentation, government or standards body, original paper,
   publisher record, primary dataset, or direct organization statement.
3. Use one available backend deliberately. Do not infer backend choice from a
   keyword list or silently fall back between paid services when credentials,
   cost, coverage, or privacy differs.
4. Retrieve only enough sources to resolve the question and its main conflict.
   Verify publication/event dates and distinguish current facts from historical
   context.
5. Compare sources when they disagree, state the evidence boundary, and identify
   any inference made by PaperTrace rather than by the sources.
6. Return the cited answer directly. Save raw results only when requested or when
   the retrieval protocol is itself part of the research contribution.

## Output Contract

Produce:

- a source-grounded answer to the bounded question;
- the load-bearing sources and access/check dates;
- disagreement, missing evidence, jurisdiction, or temporal limits;
- one concise handoff to the primary research or writing task.

## Boundaries

- Do not auto-route by promotional venue tiers, citation-count thresholds, or
  author reputation.
- Do not present generated summaries, search snippets, or model synthesis as an
  original source.
- Do not make network calls or send private material without the required user
  authorization and credentials.
- Do not silently switch backend, broaden scope, repeat paid queries, or create a
  fallback chain.
- Do not require raw export files, backend/latency narration, decision logs,
  insight logs, dead-end logs, statistics files, hashes, checksums, or receipts
  for an ordinary lookup.
- Do not write manuscript prose or promote a claim beyond what the retrieved
  sources establish.
