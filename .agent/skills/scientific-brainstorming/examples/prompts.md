# scientific-brainstorming — invocation scenarios

## Scenario 1: Structural innovation scan

> Use Card 1A. From the bottleneck in the intake, generate six non-equivalent
> IDEA-* candidates across at least three applicable M1–M5 families. Update
> `paper/kickstart/idea_candidates.yaml`. Each candidate must state the changed
> research object, before→after transformation, one primary P-pattern, a
> provisional nearest-neighbour Ref ID, and a bounded kill test. Downgrade
> module/loss/scale-only ideas. Do not formalize or approve any candidate.

Expected: compact candidate records, unresolved literature questions, no claims,
experiments, or prose.

## Scenario 2: Reopen a failed direction under a changed assumption

> Read the relevant dead-end reason and verified reading-matrix rows. Use Card
> 1A to determine whether the old direction becomes a genuinely different
> candidate under the new data constraint. Create at most three candidates,
> preserve the old rejection reason, and explain the new core-object change.
> Keep all candidates proposed unless the five gate sketches are complete.

Expected: new or eliminated candidate records with explicit relation to the old
failure; no silent deletion or novelty claim.
