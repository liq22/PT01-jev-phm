# Scientific Writing

## Purpose

Turn stable scientific content into readable prose. This supporting Skill does
not create evidence, change claim strength, or compress away technical content.

For argument structure, consult only the relevant subsection of
[Paper construction](../../references/research_first_paper_loop.md#paper-construction).
It owns the WHY–WHAT–HOW extension, section-specific logic, and experiment-writing
examples; do not maintain a second framework here.

## Workflow

1. Identify the paragraph's intellectual job and bottom line.
2. Identify the supplied claim, mechanism, condition, observation/result,
   engineering example, source, and boundary that must remain.
3. State the bottom line early, then add only the reasoning and detail needed to
   support it. Use the argument structure as a reasoning aid, not a fixed sentence
   count, repeated disclaimer, or set of literal WHY/WHAT/HOW headings.
4. Prefer domain-specific nouns and verbs over generic `evidence`, `object`,
   `framework`, `lens`, `witness`, `handoff`, or `authority` when the actual
   quantity or operation is known.
5. Keep review/search/coding procedure in Methods or supplementary material; do
   not turn repository snapshots or workflow states into scientific findings.
6. Improve local flow and remove repetition without deleting unique scientific
   information.
7. Compare the result with the supplied content once.

## Output Contract

Produce concise manuscript-ready prose whose claims, examples, equations,
numbers, citations, terminology, and boundaries match the supplied scientific
content.

## Boundaries

- Do not add volume, headings, lists, abstractions, or disclaimers merely to
  appear scholarly.
- Do not silently remove a mechanism, example, source, negative result, or
  limitation.
- Do not expose internal IDs, paths, states, corpus snapshots, promotion rules,
  gates, ledgers, or Agent workflow language.
- Do not use Python to measure prose quality or enforce paragraph form.
- Do not change conclusion strength without new scientific support.
