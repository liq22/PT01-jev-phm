# Closest Sources and Transfer Decisions

## 1. Review scope

### 1.1 Academic collection: 12 October 2026

This review serves the PHMFactory + Qwen3-0.6B study, continuing the scientific manuscript in PR #2 at `706382a0b13407038b82abda1047d091413c42cc`. It contains 50 distinct academic works: 10 already present in the manuscript bibliography and 40 added to the study collection. Existing works are 01, 03, 11, 12, 21, 22, 32, 41, 42 and 45. Publication and preprint versions count once. DeepProbLog is represented by its extended journal version only. Works 06 and 05 are related earlier/later studies, not two independent replications.

This is not a collection of 50 direct Jev-PHM applications. Vendor announcements, official implementation guidance and community evaluations remain auxiliary sources and do not count toward the 50. Finding a paper does not imply that its full methods or experiments have been verified. The reading-scope column distinguishes inspected method/experiment passages from metadata and abstract review. A failed full-text retrieval does not establish that a mechanism is absent.

The ten highest-priority readings are 03, 05, 07, 12, 13, 15, 22, 35, 41 and 42. The focused 13 October follow-up below updates the closest-neighbor scopes, including work 04. No result reported by another paper is treated as a reproduced PHM result in this project.

The phishing source's direct verdict on all 2,000 examples and regression on held-out half B have different fitting/evaluation contexts. Its matched Haiku comparison did not reject the accuracy difference at the reported p=0.063; this is not proof of equivalence. Questions were informed by the URL taxonomy and bodies were synthetic. The spam source acknowledges exploratory development, unknown public-corpus pretraining exposure, duplicate/source concerns and a phishing-only later set. Neither community result supplies PHM evidence. These source conclusions remain historical assessments, not independent reruns.

## 3. Decisions for the current paper

### 3.1 Retain the scientific scope and strengthen the comparison question

Keep the E0/E1 representation and calibration contrasts distinct from E2. The question is whether question-conditioned probabilistic decisions yield an increment on actual PHM observations beyond a numerical predictor, hierarchy on the same Qwen representation, and a same-question-count control. The 50 works are a study collection, not modules to assemble or citations to force into the Introduction.

Work 04 is direct probabilistic/LLM/calibration prior art with its original method now inspected; the remaining distinction is independent question evaluation versus sequential arbitration, not the mere presence of probabilities or an LLM. Works 03, 12, 41, 42, 45 and 48 constrain generic first-ever calibration, dual-head, hierarchy, concept and neural-probability composition claims. Their related methods do not prove that this PHM comparison is already answered. The decisive evidence remains a matched real-data result.

### 3.2 Verify the observation and independent unit before running

Works 05 and 09 make physical-unit identity a concrete prerequisite. Keep all dependent recordings of the same bearing linked across operating conditions. Tuning and calibration must respect unit disjointness as well. If healthy independent units cannot support four roles, stop the present protocol or explicitly revise it before prediction; do not treat four healthy files as four healthy bearings.

Works 02, 07, 13, 14 and 16 motivate inspecting representation adequacy rather than assuming that six numbers serialized into text preserve the fault evidence. If the initial representation fails, retain that result and propose one separate, equal-evidence revision. Do not replace features silently inside E1. Unknown geometry, speed or sampling metadata must not acquire convenient default values.

### 3.3 Separate probability quality, rejection ordering and operational decisions

Evaluate NLL/Brier and a defined ECE for probability quality; matched-coverage risk for rejection ordering; and declared scenario costs for decisions. Work 35 explicitly distinguishes TS-NLL from TS-AURC. Works 37–40 require their respective assumptions; they do not grant a guarantee merely because our output is typed or empirically calibrated.

For a fixed binary classifier with positive global temperature, maximum probability is

$$
c_T(x)=\sigma\!\left(\frac{|z_1(x)-z_0(x)|}{T}\right),\qquad T>0.
$$

It is monotone in the absolute logit gap. Temperature therefore preserves sample confidence ordering, so its risk–coverage curve at the same coverage is unchanged, using the same tie convention. NLL, coverage at a fixed probability threshold and scenario cost may still change. This elementary inference must not be generalized to arbitrary multiclass confidence rankings. It is a useful result check, not a new theorem claimed by this paper.

### 3.4 Decomposition does not add diagnostic information by itself

Our fault-presence and conditional-identity labels are deterministic transformations of the same original label. The chain

$$
p(H\mid s)=1-r(s),\qquad p(k\mid s)=r(s)u_k(s)
$$

is probability factorization, not new supervision or a physical explanation. Any value must come from representation/parameterization, learning bias, or measured decision behavior. Retain numerical-flat/factorized, Qwen-flat/factorized, Qwen-shared-factorized on the flat representation, and the same-question-count direct control. Original per-window weights are retained in the fault-only branch so that its data-fit objective remains weighted leaf NLL; branch means are not added with an unverified equivalence claim. Do not expand experiments merely because more literature is available.

### 3.5 Execution and stopping point

The authorized execution now implements and tests E2 in the existing source, then runs the frozen PHM/Qwen comparisons and completes the manuscript from actual artifacts. E0/E1 are milestones rather than the paper's stopping condition. The observed-state revision and parameterized full-record coverage are declared before test inspection; each split selects the common window using only its Numerical-flat development data. Overlap adds measurements, not independent bearings. No efficacy result is supplied by this source review. Further reading stops when load-bearing overlap is resolved; the bibliography remains limited to actually cited works.
