Finding a paper does not imply that its full methods or experiments have been verified. The reading-scope column distinguishes inspected method/experiment passages from metadata and abstract review. A failed full-text retrieval does not establish that a mechanism is absent.

The ten highest-priority readings are 03, 05, 07, 12, 13, 15, 22, 35, 41 and 42. Work 04 is a directly related preprint whose full implementation and results still need inspection. No result reported by another paper is treated as a reproduced PHM result in this project.

## 3. Decisions for the current paper

### 3.1 Retain the scientific scope and strengthen the comparison question

Keep the implemented E0/E1 probe and the designed E2 contrast. The question is whether question-conditioned probabilistic decisions yield a useful increment on actual PHM observations beyond a strong numerical predictor, an ordinary hierarchy and a same-question-count control. The 50 works are a study collection, not 50 modules to assemble or 50 citations to force into the Introduction.

Work 04 is direct probabilistic/LLM/calibration prior art but remains abstract-level here; do not claim that its unread full text omits a mechanism. Works 03, 12, 41, 42, 45 and 48 further constrain generic first-ever calibration, dual-head, hierarchy, concept and neural-probability composition claims. Their related methods do not prove that the present PHM question is already answered either. The next decisive evidence is still a real matched comparison.

### 3.2 Verify the observation and independent unit before running

Works 05 and 09 make physical-unit identity a concrete prerequisite. Keep all dependent recordings of the same bearing linked across operating conditions. Tuning and calibration must respect unit disjointness as well. If healthy independent units cannot support four roles, stop the present protocol or explicitly revise it before prediction; do not treat four healthy files as four healthy bearings.

Works 02, 07, 13, 14 and 16 motivate inspecting representation adequacy rather than assuming that six numbers serialized into text preserve the fault evidence. If the initial representation fails, retain that result and propose one separate, equal-evidence revision. Do not replace features silently inside E1. Unknown geometry, speed or sampling metadata must not acquire convenient default values.

### 3.3 Separate probability quality, rejection ordering and operational decisions

Evaluate NLL/Brier and a defined ECE for probability quality; matched-coverage risk for rejection ordering; and declared scenario costs for decisions. Work 35 explicitly distinguishes TS-NLL from TS-AURC. Works 37–40 require their respective assumptions; they do not grant a guarantee merely because our output is typed or empirically calibrated.

For a fixed binary classifier with positive global temperature, maximum probability is

$$
c_T(x)=\sigma\!\left(\frac{|z_1(x)-z_0(x)|}{T}\right),\qquad T>0.
$$

It is monotone in the absolute logit gap. Temperature therefore preserves sample confidence ordering, so its risk–coverage curve at the same coverage is unchanged, using the same tie convention. NLL, coverage at a fixed probability threshold and scenario cost may still change. This elementary inference must not be generalized to arbitrary multiclass confidence rankings. It is a useful result check, not a new theorem claimed by this paper.

### 3.4 Decomposition does not add diagnostic information by itself

Our fault-presence and conditional-identity labels are deterministic transformations of the same original label. The chain

$$
p(H\mid s)=1-r(s),\qquad p(k\mid s)=r(s)u_k(s)
$$

is probability factorization, not new supervision or a physical explanation. Any value must come from a useful representation/parameterization, learning bias or measured decision behavior. Retain numerical-flat/factorized, Qwen-flat/factorized and the same-question-count direct control already specified in E2. Do not expand the experiment queue merely because more literature is available.

### 3.5 Handoff and stopping point

Complete the existing real PHMFactory metadata/grouping and Qwen forward-pass E0, then execute the frozen E1. E2 remains design-only until implemented and tested in the existing source. This review did not run PHM experiments, add Results, introduce new runtime code or claim improved performance. Further full-text reading should target only a material nearest-neighbor, representation or validity uncertainty. The manuscript bibliography remains limited to cited works; this reading matrix holds the broader study collection.
